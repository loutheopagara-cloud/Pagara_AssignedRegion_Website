Welcome to SOCCSKSARGEN

SOCCSKSARGEN is a region in the southern part of Mindanao, Philippines. It is known for its beautiful natural attractions, diverse cultures, delicious local products, and friendly communities. From the peaceful waters of Lake Sebu to the majestic Mount Matutum, the region offers many places to explore and unforgettable experiences for every visitor.

Explore Popular Tourist Spots

Lake Sebu

Discover the peaceful lake and the rich traditions of the T'boli people.

Seven Falls

Experience the beauty of cascading waterfalls surrounded by green forests.

Asik-Asik Falls

Admire the unique waterfall flowing from a lush, plant-covered rock wall.

Mount Matutum

Enjoy the impressive volcanic peak and its surrounding natural landscape.

Kalon Barak

Take in scenic mountain views and the peaceful atmosphere of the countryside.

Sarangani Bay

Explore beautiful coastal scenery and enjoy the natural beauty of the bay.
