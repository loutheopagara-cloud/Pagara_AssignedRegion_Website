Brief History of SOCCSKSARGEN

SOCCSKSARGEN is a region in the southern part of Mindanao, Philippines. Its name comes from South Cotabato, Cotabato, Sultan Kudarat, Sarangani, and General Santos City. The region is known for its rich natural resources, agriculture, and cultural diversity.

Before the arrival of many settlers, different Indigenous communities, including the T'boli, B'laan, and Manobo, lived in the area. They developed unique traditions, languages, clothing, music, and ways of life that remain important parts of the region's identity.

Over time, migration and trade brought different communities together, creating a diverse population with various customs and beliefs. Today, SOCCSKSARGEN continues to celebrate its cultural heritage while developing its agriculture, tourism, and local industries.

Cultural Highlights

Festivals

  • T'nalak Festival – celebrates T'boli culture and traditional weaving.
  • Kalivungan Festival – celebrates the culture and unity of Cotabato.
  • Gensan Tuna Festival – celebrates General Santos City's tuna industry.
  • Hinugyaw Festival – showcases the culture and traditions of Koronadal City.

Traditional Crafts

  • T'nalak weaving – traditional abaca cloth made by T'boli weavers.
  • Beadwork – colorful accessories and decorations made by Indigenous communities.
  • Traditional embroidery – decorative designs on cultural clothing.
  • Handcrafted accessories – locally made cultural ornaments and crafts.

Traditional Foods

  • Tuna dishes – popular seafood in General Santos City.
  • Pastil – rice wrapped in banana leaves with seasoned meat.
  • Native delicacies – traditional sweets and rice-based snacks.
  • Grilled seafood – fresh fish and other seafood prepared in local styles.
